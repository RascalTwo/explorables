Spaced repetition in a tab — no install, no account. Answer out loud or by typing; the scheduler (FSRS, the same algorithm Anki uses) decides when you next see each card. Nothing leaves your machine — the deck you load and the progress you build both stay in this browser, and speech is recognised on-device. Press Demo deck to see the format, or load your own JSON.
