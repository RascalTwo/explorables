Coding Challenge Walkthroughs

Coding challenges as interactive walkthroughs — from freeCodeCamp's dailies and LeetCode. Each is the problem, the visual intuition, the gotcha, and idiomatic TypeScript; every tab is badged by source with a link to the original. Where a problem has a naive way and a clever way, flip the Approach toggle to watch the brute-force version strain and the optimized one glide. Pick a tab. Poke at everything.

freeCodeCamp's daily challenge ran for exactly one year — #1 Vowel Balance on 11 Aug 2025 through #365 The Last Challenge: Bucket Fill 3 on 10 Aug 2026 — and no longer publishes new ones; the dailies now replay from the start. So this gallery covers a finished set rather than chasing a moving one: #293 to #365 is complete, every day of it, and the back-fill into #1–292 has begun.
